# Flappy Vela

The one-button arcade classic, drawn for the round
screen, with Vela the puppy in the bird's place. She
wears a propeller hat, her own little wind device:
it spins hard while she climbs and idles while she
glides, and her tongue comes out on the way down.

Tap anywhere to flap: tap to start, tap to keep her
in the air, tap to play again. Pipes slide in from
the right with a gap to thread; each pipe cleared is
a point, and the pipes come a little faster with
every point. Hitting a pipe knocks her out of the
sky; the run ends there or when she reaches the
ground. The best score is kept in the app store.

One 400x400 canvas at zoom 1 in partial mode. The
scene is painted once, then each frame covers the
pipes' and Vela's old spots and draws them at the new
ones. The score at the top hugs its digits, so it
repaints as a number, not a banner. Vela herself is
drawn from circles and lines, ear, snout and
propeller and all.
