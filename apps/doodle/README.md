# Vela Jump

A doodle-jump climber, drawn for the round screen,
with Vela the puppy in the doodler's place and her
propeller hat spinning at every bounce. She bounces
on platforms forever; the only way is up.

Touch and drag to steer her left and right; she wraps
around the edges of the screen. Green platforms are
steady, blue ones slide sideways, brown ones break
under her feet and drop away. Some green platforms
carry a spring: land on it for a real launch. The
score is the height climbed, in metres, and the run
ends when she falls off the bottom of the screen. The
best height is kept in the app store.

One 400x400 canvas at zoom 1 in partial mode. The
scene is painted once, then each frame covers the
platforms' and Vela's old spots and draws them at the
new ones; the height at the top repaints only when it
changes or something passes behind it.
